## Scout — Investment Brief

**Batch:** Winter 2022  |  **Score:** 72.7/100  |  **Verdict:** Strong — pursue

### What they do
Scout is building a home diagnostic testing platform to fill a gap in digital health—enabling patients to collect medical-grade test data at home. Their solution includes proprietary hardware (test kits and Hub device), software (app), and claims 10x lower development costs and 3x lower per-test pricing than existing approaches while meeting clinical performance standards. The customer base appears to be healthcare providers and organizations seeking to deliver remote diagnostic capabilities to patients.

### Market opportunity
Home-based diagnostics is a natural extension of telehealth adoption accelerated by COVID-19, addressing a real infrastructure gap. The market is large but fragmented—no dominant player yet owns the home testing space at scale. Tailwinds include continued pressure on providers to enable remote care and consumer appetite for convenience, though data on current market size and growth rates is not provided.

The low funding velocity score (4.0/10) suggests difficulty raising capital, which is concerning for a hardware-software company with long sales cycles. The press coverage provided appears to be false positives (unrelated articles about scouts in sports/crime), raising questions about data quality.
