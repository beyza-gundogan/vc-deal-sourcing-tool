### What they do
Clarion builds AI agents that handle the high volume of inbound calls and messages that clinics receive daily—scheduling, billing, prescription refills, and similar tasks. The product automates these workflows end-to-end, freeing clinical staff to focus on patient care rather than administrative overhead. They're already processing tens of thousands of patient interactions monthly.

### Market opportunity
Healthcare clinics miss 30-40% of inbound calls due to understaffing, representing a material operational problem across the industry. The healthcare AI market is growing rapidly as labor shortages persist and providers seek efficiency gains. This is a well-timed entry into a large, fragmented market with clear pain points and obvious ROI for customers.
